The four dividend dates

  1. Declaration date — the board announces the dividend amount.
  2. Ex-dividend date — buy on or after this date and you do not get the dividend; the price typically opens lower by roughly the dividend amount.
  3. Record date — the company checks its shareholder register; you must already be a holder.
  4. Payment date — the money lands in your bank account.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good dividend yield?

Indian large-caps typically yield 1–3%; utilities and PSUs often more. A very high yield can be a warning sign — it may reflect a falling share price rather than a generous company.

Are dividends taxable?

In India, dividends are added to your income and taxed at your slab rate, with TDS deducted above a threshold. Rules change — confirm current law or ask a tax professional.
