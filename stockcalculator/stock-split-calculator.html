What a split changes

Your share count multiplies, the price divides by the same factor, and your holding's value is unchanged. More detail: face value splits & demergers.

Frequently asked questions

Do I lose money in a stock split?

No. If you held 10 shares at ₹1,000 (₹10,000) and the stock splits 1:5, you now hold 50 shares at ₹200 — still ₹10,000. Only the unit size changes.
