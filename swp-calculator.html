How an SWP works

You withdraw a fixed amount monthly while the rest keeps earning. Withdraw less than the corpus earns and it lasts forever; more, and the "corpus lasts" figure shows when it runs out.

Frequently asked questions

What withdrawal rate makes a corpus last forever?

If the monthly withdrawal is no more than the corpus times the monthly return rate, the corpus never shrinks. For example, at 8% per annum a corpus of ₹50 lakh sustains about ₹33,000 per month indefinitely before inflation.

Does this account for inflation?

No — it models a fixed withdrawal. In real life you will likely need to increase withdrawals with inflation, which shortens how long the corpus lasts. Plan with a buffer.
