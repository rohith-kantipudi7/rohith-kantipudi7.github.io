The EMI formula

EMI = P × i × (1 + i)n ÷ [(1 + i)n − 1]

P = principal, i = monthly rate, n = months. Always compare the total interest, not just the EMI.

Frequently asked questions

How can I reduce my total interest?

Three levers: a shorter tenure (higher EMI, far less interest), part-prepayments early in the loan when the interest share is highest, and refinancing to a lower rate.
