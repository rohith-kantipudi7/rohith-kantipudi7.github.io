Why 72?

Years to double ≈ 72 ÷ annual return %

72 divides cleanly by 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9 and 12, making mental math easy, and it closely tracks the exact answer ln 2 ÷ ln(1 + r) for typical return rates. At 8% the rule says 9 years; the exact figure is 9.01. The approximation drifts at very high rates — the calculator shows both.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is the Rule of 72?

Very accurate between roughly 4% and 15% annual returns — within a few weeks of the exact answer. Outside that band it drifts; this page shows the exact logarithmic result alongside for comparison.
