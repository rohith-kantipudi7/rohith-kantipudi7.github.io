The SIP formula

FV = P × [((1 + i)n − 1) ÷ i] × (1 + i)

P = monthly amount, i = monthly rate (annual ÷ 12), n = number of instalments. Starting early matters more than starting big.

Frequently asked questions

Is the SIP return guaranteed?

No. The calculator projects a constant assumed return; real market returns vary year to year. Use a conservative rate for planning — many investors model equity SIPs at 10–12% per annum.

What happens if I increase my SIP every year?

A step-up SIP (increasing the instalment annually) can significantly raise the final corpus. This calculator models a fixed instalment; add a margin or recalculate with a higher monthly amount to approximate a step-up.
