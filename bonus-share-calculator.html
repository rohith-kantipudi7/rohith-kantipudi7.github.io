How a bonus issue works

A bonus issue capitalises the company's reserves into new shares handed free to existing shareholders. Your invested amount is unchanged, so your average cost per share drops in proportion. Note that fractional entitlements are usually settled in cash — this calculator rounds bonus shares down to whole shares, as exchanges do.

For announced bonus issues and record dates, see bonus & buyback details.

Frequently asked questions

Is a bonus share free money?

Not quite — the share price adjusts down on the ex-bonus date so the total value of your holding stays the same. The benefit is more liquid units and a signal of confidence from the company.
