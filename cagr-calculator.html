Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between CAGR and absolute return?

Absolute return ignores time: doubling your money is 100% whether it took 2 years or 20. CAGR annualises it — doubling in 2 years is a 41.4% CAGR; in 20 years it is only 3.5%.
