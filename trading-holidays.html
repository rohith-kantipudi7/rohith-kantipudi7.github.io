Trading Holidays 2026 (NSE & BSE)

All days the Indian equity markets are closed in 2026, plus Muhurat trading. Always confirm near the date on the NSE site — exchanges occasionally revise the list.

Muhurat trading

Sunday, 8 November 2026 (Diwali — Laxmi Pujan): markets hold a special one-hour Muhurat trading session; exact timings are notified by the exchanges closer to the date.

Holidays falling on weekends

Mahashivratri (15 Feb, Sun), Id-Ul-Fitr (21 Mar, Sat), Independence Day (15 Aug, Sat), and Diwali Laxmi Pujan (8 Nov, Sun) fall on non-trading days in 2026.

Verify before you plan: the official calendar lives at NSE — exchange holidays. Exchanges can add or revise holidays during the year.

Frequently asked questions

Are NSE and BSE holidays the same?

Almost always yes for equity trading — both follow the same list of trading holidays. Settlement holidays and commodity-market (MCX) timings can differ.
